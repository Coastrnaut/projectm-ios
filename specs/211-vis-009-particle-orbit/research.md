# Research: VIS-009 Particle Orbit

- **VIS-009-D1 Bass input.** ANL-007's `AnalysisFrame.kickEnvelope` (1 on a 40–150 Hz transient, decaying) already reaches `NativeSpectrumHost.kickEnvelope` every frame and MOD-002's `.kick` source. It is the default "bass". Users wanting a smoother bass bind e.g. MOD-015's `audio.bandEnvelope.medium.0` to the radius parameter. Alternatives: the raw lowest band (level-dependent, no decay shape); a new analysis feature (out of scope).
- **VIS-009-D2 Binding path.** `AudioFeatureModulator.value(for:manual:)` returns the manual value when unbound, else the MOD-011 fold kept in range. `ParticleOrbitRadiusDrive.resolve(manual:kick:modulator:)` returns (base, bass) = bound ? (value, 0) : (manual, kick). Pure, no allocation (the modulator's lookup is a linear scan of ≤ 32 slots).
- **VIS-009-D3 Radius slew.** One-pole step a = 1 − exp(−Δt/τ) precomputed per configuration; Δr = clamp(a·(target − r), ±rate·Δt). For a constant target the radius converges geometrically: after 0.5 s (120 steps, τ 0.06 s) the residual is < e^-8 ≈ 3e-4 of the distance, inside the 1 % acceptance. Monotonic in the target: for a fixed start, a larger constant target gives a larger radius at every step (both the one-pole term and the clamp are monotone).
- **VIS-009-D4 Stepping.** VIS-008's schedule: step k starts at anchor + ⌊k·10⁹/240⌋ ns; ≤ 240 steps per advance; at exact 60 Hz frame times (⌊n·10⁹/60⌋) each frame runs exactly 4 steps. Beats are applied at the first step starting at or after their time.
- **VIS-009-D5 Work bound.** The simulation counts particle steps (`stepsRun × count`) and the layout reports squares; the test asserts per-frame deltas ≤ 1,024 and ≤ 257 squares (1,542 vertices) for 600 frames at 256 particles, plus unchanged storage addresses. `measure` reports a baseline only.
- **VIS-009-D6 Flash model.** Squares as in VIS-008 (axis-aligned, clipped by `SpectrumFlashAreas`); the centre square is region 0. Constant particle alpha 0.75 and centre alpha 0.7 keep the picture's light steady; the pulse changes only the centre's area.
- **VIS-009-D7 Golden.** Seed 0x5EED0009, 160 particles, base 0.5, steady bass 0.5 from t = 0, one beat at t = 0.2 s, rendered at t = 0.5 s from 60 Hz frames; glow off, Reduce flashing off; 128×96 and 256×192.
