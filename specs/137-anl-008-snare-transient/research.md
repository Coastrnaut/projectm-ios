# Research: ANL-008 Mid Transient Detector

- **ANL-008-D1 Reuse ANL-007's detector.** Decision: a configuration, not a new type. ANL-007-R9 designed `BandTransientDetector` for this; its level flux, median + k·MAD threshold, floor, dominance and refractory rules are band-agnostic. Alternatives: a mid-band copy of ANL-004's flux (level-dependent through its −70 dB normalization; full-band design), a classifier (asserts identity).
- **ANL-008-D2 Confidence.** Decision: √(margin · breadth′), margin = clamp((rise − threshold)/12 dB, 0, 1), breadth = clamp(flatness/0.5, 0, 1) over the band's bin powers on the event frame (flatness = exp(mean ln p) / mean p, powers floored at 1e-20), breadth′ = 1 − w + w·breadth. 12 dB: a rise one power-×16 above the threshold is unambiguous; a kick's first frame clears its threshold by 7–36 dB (ANL-007 finding), so margins span the scale. 0.5: a Hann-windowed white-noise frame has a flatness near 0.5–0.6 (exponentially distributed bin powers give e^−γ ≈ 0.56), so noise-like attacks read ≈ 1 and a sinusoid (one peak plus leakage) reads near 0. Geometric mean: both conditions must hold for a confident event. Kick weight 0 keeps the kick unchanged. Alternatives: arithmetic mean (a tone with a big margin would read 0.5+), band crest factor (needs a reference spectrum).
- **ANL-008-D3 Band and timing.** 1–4 kHz; 70 ms refractory; 80 ms envelope (spec Clarifications).
- **ANL-008-D4 Exposure.** One source reading envelope × held confidence; confidence also on the event and the frame. Opcode 17 (next free value after ANL-014's 16).
- **ANL-008-D5 Fixtures.** Mid-band noise: seeded white noise, one-pole low-pass (≈ 1 kHz), ring-modulated by a 2 kHz carrier (energy ≈ 1–3 kHz), decaying envelope (τ 30 ms, 120 ms long). Kick: ANL-007's 60 Hz decaying sine bursts. Tone: a 2 kHz sine. All start on hop boundaries; sample clocks only.
