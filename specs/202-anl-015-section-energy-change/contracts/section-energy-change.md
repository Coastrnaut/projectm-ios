# Contract: ANL-015 Section Energy Change

- **C1 Slow level.** `SectionChangeDetector.process(levelDecibels:frameEnd:)` clamps the level to ≥ floor (non-finite → floor), stores its power 10^(L/10) in a ring of `windowFrames` entries, and sets the slow level to 10·log10(mean of the stored powers) (fewer entries early in a session: the mean of those present). Every value is finite.
- **C2 Changes.** While `frameEnd < settleSamples` the reference equals the slow level and nothing is pending. Afterwards: idle and |S − R| ≥ enter → pending in that direction, start = frameEnd, R frozen; pending and (S − R)·sign ≤ hold → idle; pending and frameEnd − start ≥ dwellSamples → returns a `SectionChange`, R ← S, refractory until frameEnd + refractorySamples. While idle or refractory R ← R + c·(S − R). In refractory nothing becomes pending. Results depend only on the sequence of (level, frameEnd); no clock, allocation, lock or log.
- **C3 Envelope.** 1 on a frame that returns a change; otherwise multiplied by `envelopeDecay` per frame and set to 0 below 0.001; always within 0…1.
- **C4 Pipeline.** `AnalysisPipeline` runs the detector on every frame with ANL-014's level and the frame's window end; the frame carries `sectionChangeEnvelope` and `sectionLevelDecibels`; a change is published as `AnalysisEvent(kind: .sectionChange(...))` with `time` = the frame's timestamp and `eventSample` = `startSample`, after the frame's transient events. A session start resets the detector. `AnalysisPipelineConfiguration(sectionChange:)` must share the spectrum's sample rate and hop (`sampleRateMismatch`).
- **C5 Source.** `AudioFeatureSource.sectionChange.id == "audio.sectionChange"`, parses back; value = `AudioFeatureFrame.sectionChange` clamped to 0…1 (nil or non-finite → 0). `CompiledExpression("audio.sectionChange")` emits opcode 35. `inspectorName == "Section change"`; the native visual feeds the envelope to its bindings.
