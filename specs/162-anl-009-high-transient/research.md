# Research: ANL-009 High-Frequency Transient Detector

- **ANL-009-D1 Reuse ANL-007's detector.** Decision: a configuration plus one general field, not a new type (ANL-008-D1's reasons; ANL-007-R9 designed the detector for it). Alternatives: a high-band copy of ANL-004's flux (level-dependent), a classifier (asserts identity).
- **ANL-009-D2 Bounding the event count.** Decision: a sliding 1 s window cap, `maximumEventsPerSecond` N; the detector keeps the sample indices of its last N events in a ring allocated in `init` (capacity N, at most 64) and fires only if the oldest of them is at least one second (round(rate) samples) before the candidate, so (sample − rate, sample] never holds more than N events. A suppressed candidate is handled like a refractory frame: the detector disarms, nothing else changes, `rateLimitedEvents` counts it. N = 12: sixteenth notes at 180 BPM = 12/s pass; dense rolls, noise and cymbal washes are limited. Measured: at the bus's geometry (2,048 / 512) the level reference spans four frames, so evenly spaced clicks re-fire at most about every 7 hops (≈ 12.3/s), and 50/s clicks or continuous noise give one event; at 512 / 128, 20/s clicks fire 40 times in 2 s uncapped (21 in one second) and 24 capped (12 per second). Alternatives: a longer refractory period (blocks fast fills and does not bound irregular input more tightly than its own spacing), a token bucket (allows bursts above N; harder to state as a bound), a per-frame probability (non-deterministic).
- **ANL-009-D3 Band, dominance and timing.** 6–16 kHz (spec Clarifications). Dominance −6 dB: a one-pole low-passed 1 kHz noise ring-modulated to 2 kHz (ANL-008's mid fixture) puts about −10 to −12 dB of its power increase above 6 kHz (∫ of 1/(1 + (f/1 kHz)²) above 4 kHz is ≈ 11 % of the total); with ANL-007's −15 dB it fired 4 of 4 with confidence 0.97–1.00, with −6 dB it fires 0. High-pass clicks hold ≈ −2 dB and white clicks ≈ −3.4 dB of their increase in the band. Refractory 40 ms, envelope 50 ms.
- **ANL-009-D4 Exposure.** As ANL-008-D4: one source reading envelope × held confidence. Opcode 18, the next free value after ANL-008's 17 (checked across every `feature/*` branch on 2026-11-14).
- **ANL-009-D5 Fixtures.** High-band clicks: seeded white noise through two cascaded one-pole high-passes at 7 kHz (12 dB/octave below), exponential decay (τ 8 ms over 30 ms for the acceptance; τ 2 ms over 10 ms for dense trains), peak-normalized, on hop-aligned starts. Continuous noise: the same filter, 10 s. Kick: ANL-007's 60 Hz decaying sine bursts. Mid: ANL-008's mid-band noise bursts. Sample clocks only; a 1 s span counter over event sample indices.
