# External display and output tools

Connect a display, projector or AirPlay receiver and the visualization appears there. Settings › **External Display** changes only the picture on that display.

## Fitting the picture

- **Fit** shows the whole picture with bars; **Fill** covers the screen and crops; **Stretch** fills it exactly.
- **Shape** keeps the display's shape or this device's.
- **Pixel aspect** keeps circles round on screens with non-square pixels.
- **Resolution** sets how many pixels are drawn for the display.
- **Output**: **Same scene** shows the scene as composed, logo included; **Clean output** shows the visual only.

## Projection tools

- **Corner pin…** moves the picture's four corners to fit a projector that is not square to the screen. A test card helps you line up the edges.
- **Grid warp…** bends the picture with a grid of points (3 × 3 to 9 × 9) to fit a curved or uneven surface. Guide lines on the display help you line up the grid. Shapes that would fold the picture are refused.
- **Color calibration…** adjusts gamma and red, green and blue for that display only.
- **Rehearse output…** previews a display that is not connected yet. Nothing there is saved.
- **Test grid** and the safe-area guides help with framing.

## During a performance

- **Black out output** makes the output black at once; **Restore output** brings it back.
- **Performance layout** in the controls shows large buttons for the previous and next cue, tap tempo and blackout.
- Output profiles can be exported and imported with Files.

## Good to know

While the visualization is on an external display, this device shows the controls, not the visualization. Output diagnostics in Settings show what the display reports.
