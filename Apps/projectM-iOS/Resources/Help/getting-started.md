# Getting started and audio sources

projectM turns sound into moving pictures. Nothing starts until you choose an audio source.

## Choosing a source

When the app opens it shows **Choose Audio Source**. Pick one of three sources:

- **System/App Capture** visualizes the sound of content you choose to share, where iOS allows it. iOS shows its own picker and recording indicator, and you can stop at any time. It needs a newer iOS version on some devices; the chooser says so when it is not available. Whether a particular app's sound can be shared is decided by iOS and by that app, so it may be silent.
- **Microphone** visualizes sound in the room. iOS asks for microphone access the first time, after a short explanation.
- **In-App Playback** plays the built-in reference track, test signals, or audio files you import with **Import Audio…**. It opens no input.

## While it runs

Tap the picture to show or hide the controls. **Switch Source** returns to the chooser; **Pause** and **Stop** do what they say. The status line under the picture says when audio is arriving, silent, or has stopped, and why.

## Good to know

- Audio is processed in memory on this device only.
- If the picture stops moving, check the status line first: the source may have been ended by iOS, for example when the device locks.
- **Settings** in the controls holds every option described in these articles. **Done** closes it.
