# Accessibility and sensory safety

Visualizers can flash. These settings make the picture calmer, and they apply to every visual.

## Reduce flashing

Settings › **Reduce flashing** limits rapid full-screen brightness changes. **Follow Reduce Motion** keeps it in step with the iOS Reduce Motion setting until you change it yourself. On a device that uses the fallback renderer, the app says that Reduce flashing is not available.

## Sensory safety

Settings › **Sensory safety** sets a **Profile**: **Standard**, **Reduced**, **Strict** or **Custom**. It limits how often the picture may flash, how fast it may brighten (**Brightness rise limit**) and how strong glow and music-driven movement may get (**Intensity limit**). Reduced and Strict apply to every visual; projectM presets keep their own motion. Presets marked **May flash** are worth avoiding if flashing affects you.

## Other help

- **Haptic beat pulses** let you feel the beat; they follow the intensity limit and stop under Strict.
- **Color-blind-safe colors** draws the spectrum bars from blue-gray to yellow instead of a rainbow.
- One-handed and left-handed layouts move the controls within reach.
- VoiceOver reads every control, and value changes are announced. Text follows your Dynamic Type size.

## Important

If flashing lights or patterns affect you, use Strict and Reduce flashing, and stop watching if you feel unwell. No setting can remove every risk.
