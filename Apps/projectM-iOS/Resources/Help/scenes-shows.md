# Scenes and shows

A **scene** is the current look: the visual, its options, its layers and effects. A **show** is an ordered list of cues, each of which recalls a scene.

## Scenes

- **Scenes** in the controls (shown once your show has cues, or Command-K on a keyboard) lists the show's scenes; search them or filter by tag.
- **Save Snapshot** in Settings keeps the current state so you can return to it.
- **Duplicate Scene**, **Export Scene…** and **Import Scene…** copy scenes, also between devices through Files. A scene pack carries the photos, slides, logo and video it uses and never needs the internet.
- Settings has **Undo** and **Redo** for scene changes.
- **Lock scene** prevents accidental edits during a performance; unlocking asks first.
- **Guided first scene** in Settings walks you through making one.

## Shows

Settings › **Show** opens the show editor.

1. Set up a look, then tap **Add Current Scene** to add it as a cue.
2. Reorder cues, add notes, and give cues a dwell time or a transition.
3. During the show, **Next** and **Previous** (or the arrow keys on a keyboard) step through the cues.

Keyframe tracks change one value along the show timeline. **Rotate scenes** can move through scenes on its own.

## Good to know

Scenes and shows are stored on this device and in your library backups, never online.
