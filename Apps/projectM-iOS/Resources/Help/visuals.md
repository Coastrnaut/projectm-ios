# Visuals

Settings › **Visual** chooses what is drawn. Only one visual shows at a time.

## projectM

The **projectM** visual runs the classic preset engine: thousands of presets, each its own look, that react to the music. Use **Next**, **Previous** and **Random** in the controls, or open **Presets** to browse. See the Presets and library article.

## Native visuals

The other visuals are drawn by the app itself and react to the same audio:

- **Circular Spectrum**, **Linear Spectrum** and **Spectrum Waterfall** show loudness by pitch.
- **Oscilloscope**, **Lissajous** and **Radial Waveform** draw the sound wave itself.
- **Beat Rings**, **Mandala**, **Particle Fountain**, **Particle Orbit**, **Noise Field**, **Kaleidoscope**, **Tunnel**, **Fluid Ink**, **Reaction Diffusion** and **Starfield** are generative scenes driven by level and beats.
- **Camera**, **Photo**, **Video** and **Text** show your own picture or words with the effects on top. See the Layers and media article.

## Options

Below the visual list, Settings shows the options of the chosen visual, such as **Bars**, **Line**, **Symmetry** or **Trails**. **Glow** adds a soft halo around bright parts. **Smoothing** sets how quickly bars rise (**Attack**) and fall (**Release**).

If a visual is not available on this device, the app says so and shows projectM instead.
