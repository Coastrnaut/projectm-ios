# Privacy

The app collects no data. It has no accounts, no analytics and no advertising.

## Audio

Audio is processed in memory on this device. It is not recorded unless you tap **Record**, and it is never sent anywhere. Audio files you import stay on this device until you remove them.

## Permissions

The app asks only when you use a feature that needs it, never at launch:

- **Microphone**, when you choose Microphone as the source;
- **Camera**, when you choose the Camera visual;
- **Local Network**, when you turn on the OSC receiver;
- **Screen recording**, by iOS's own picker each time you start System/App Capture;
- **Photos (add only)**, when you tap Save to Photos.

You can change each one later in the iOS Settings app.

## Network

The app's only network traffic is Apple's App Store traffic when you buy or restore the full preset library, and, only if you turn them on, OSC messages on your own local network. This help is part of the app and never needs a connection.

## Your files

Scenes, presets, recordings and settings stay on this device. **Storage** in Settings shows how much space they take and can clean up caches. Library backups go only where you choose to save them.
