# Effects

Effects change the finished picture of any visual. Each has its own section in Settings, and each is off or neutral until you change it.

## The effects

- **Color** grade: **Contrast**, **Temperature**, **Lift**, **Gamma**, **Gain** and **Saturation**. **Reset to neutral** puts them back.
- **Trails** leaves fading copies of moving parts; **Length** sets how long they last.
- **Chromatic split** separates red, green and blue by an **Amount** and **Angle**, or outward from the centre with **Radial**.
- **Directional blur** smears along an **Angle** by a **Radius**.
- **Radial blur** zooms outward from a focus point; set **Strength**, **Focus X** and **Focus Y**.
- **Echo** repeats the picture after a **Delay**, with a number of **Echoes** that **Fade**.

## Effect order

**Effect order** shows which effect runs first, for example Trails before Color. Tap it to reorder with **Edit**, turn effects on or off, or save the order as a chain preset. Flash safety and tone mapping always run last and cannot be moved.

## Safety

Your **Sensory safety** settings limit effects that could flash or brighten quickly; for example they can shorten Trails. See the Accessibility and sensory safety article.

## Tips

- Change one effect at a time and listen to the music with it.
- **Parameter search** in Settings finds any setting by name and opens it in the inspector.
