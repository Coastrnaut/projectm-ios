# Recording

## Save Frame

**Save Frame** in the controls saves the picture exactly as shown. You can then save it to Files, share it, or use **Save to Photos**.

## Record

**Record** makes a video of the visualization while you record; **Stop recording** ends it. Afterwards you save it to Files, share it, keep it in the app, or discard it.

- **Sound**: with In-App Playback the recording includes the app's own audio. Microphone sound is included only if you turn on Settings › Recording › **Include microphone audio** (off by default). System/App Capture sound is never recorded, so those recordings are silent.
- **Quality**, **Frame rate** and **Aspect** in Settings set the video's size and shape.
- **Countdown** can start recording after a few seconds, and a timed stop can end it for you.

## Your recordings

Recordings you keep are listed under Settings › Storage › Saved recordings. They stay on this device until you delete them. **Rename Recording** and **Save to Photos** are there too.

## Save to Photos

iOS asks for add-only access the first time. The app can only add the item; it never sees your photo library. If access is off, the file stays in the app.

## Offline export

**Export Video Offline…** in Settings renders a video from an audio file frame by frame, without playing it in real time.

## Good to know

Nothing is recorded unless you tap Record or Save Frame, and nothing is uploaded.
