# Presets and library

Presets are the looks of the **projectM** visual.

## Browsing

Tap **Presets** in the controls to open the browser. Search presets and authors, and use **Tags**, energy and colour filters to narrow the list. Tap a preset to play it.

- **Favorites** keeps the presets you like at hand.
- **Collections** are your own lists; **Add to Collection…** puts a preset in one.
- **Duplicate to My Presets** makes your own copy that you can adjust with **Adjust Parameters…** and keep in versions.
- Presets marked **May flash** can show rapid brightness changes.

## The full library

The app includes a set of presets. The **Full preset library** is a one-time in-app purchase that unlocks the rest. **Restore Purchases** brings back a purchase made with the same Apple Account. The library itself is inside the app, so browsing and playing never need a connection; only buying and restoring talk to the App Store.

## Importing

**Import Preset…** and **Import Folder or Archive…** add .milk presets from Files. Imported presets are checked before they can play, and copies of presets you already have are skipped.

## History

Settings can **Remember Recently Played** presets. It is off by default, keeps the list on this device only, and turning it off clears the list.
