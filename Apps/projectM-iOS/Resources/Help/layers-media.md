# Layers and media

Some visuals are built from **layers**: the visual itself, your media, and overlays such as a logo or a clock.

## Layers

Settings › **Layers** lists the layers from top to bottom. Choose one to see its settings: opacity, placement, a **Color Key** or **Luma Key** to cut out parts, a **Mask**, or a **Gradient**. **Solo** and **Mute** help you check one layer; they are never saved.

## Your own media

- **Camera** shows the live view of the rear or front camera behind the visualization. iOS asks for camera access the first time. **Mirror**, **Lens**, **Lock exposure** and **Lock white balance** adjust the picture. **Person mask**, **Face tracking** and **Hand tracking** work on this device only and save nothing; the tracking switches ask you to confirm first. The **Camera on** badge has a button that stops the camera at once.
- **Photo** shows one picture you choose; **Slideshow** and **Ken Burns** move through several.
- **Video** plays a clip you choose without its sound. **Speed**, **Reverse** and loop points change only the video, never the music.
- **Text** shows your own words with colour, outline and box options.

Each copy is made on this device without its location or camera details, and **Remove Photo** or **Remove Video** deletes it.

## Overlays

**Logo**, **Clock** and **Countdown** sit on top of the visuals that support them.

## Missing media

If a saved scene names a photo or video that is no longer stored, Settings › Storage › **Missing Media** helps you find it again.
