# Bundled preset licenses

Every bundled preset has one complete row here (FR-047, research R-09). `PresetRepository`
(Packages/Rendering) shows only presets with every field filled in and reports any others.

## Source

All presets come from the project owner's fork of the projectM UWP frontend,
[Coastrnaut/frontend-uwp](https://github.com/Coastrnaut/frontend-uwp), folder
`src/projectM-UWP/presets/presets_projectM-UWP` at commit `82c468ee11a535c8fb4e864cd556fc8a59bdbaf3` (owner decision 2026-11-09,
task T073). That repository's LGPL-2.1 license covers the frontend's code, not the preset files.

## License basis (`LicenseRef-Milkdrop-PublicDomain-Assumed`)

Owner decision 2026-11-09. Milkdrop presets were almost never released under a stated license;
each author in principle keeps copyright. The projectM team's preset packs (for example
[presets-cream-of-the-crop/LICENSE.md](https://github.com/projectM-visualizer/presets-cream-of-the-crop/blob/master/LICENSE.md))
treat them as public domain because they were released freely and have been redistributed widely
for two decades, and remove any preset whose author asks. This app follows the same basis and the
same policy: a preset is removed from the next release if its author objects. The author column is
taken from the Milkdrop file-name convention ("Author - Title").

## Not bundled

- `martin - sparky caleidoscope.milk` (in the fork's folder): projectM 4.2 cannot compile its
  per-frame code, because a variable name (`is_beat`) is split across `per_frame_21` and
  `per_frame_22`. Milkdrop accepted this; the file is left unedited and out of the bundle (T073,
  found by `BundledPresetsTests`).
