# Custom license agreement for App Store Connect (ADR-0005 final conclusion; T112)

Paste the text below into App Store Connect › App Information › License Agreement (custom EULA).
It keeps the terms Apple requires of a custom EULA (Apple's "Minimum Terms of Developer's End-User
License Agreement") and states that the open-source licenses govern the open-source components,
which addresses LGPL-2.1 §10 (ADR-0005, RI-007). Not legal advice; the owner approved the
conclusion it implements on 2026-11-10.
